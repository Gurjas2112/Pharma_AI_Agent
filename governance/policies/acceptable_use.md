# Acceptable use
- Research on approved molecules: new indications, dosage forms, patient populations, markets, IP, supply.
- Summarising internal strategy documents the user is entitled to see.

# Prohibited uses (enforced by guardrails/checks.py and logged)
- Synthesis of controlled or harmful substances, weapons, or evasion of regulators.
- Prompt injection, or extraction of system prompts, secrets or another user's data.
- Clinical treatment advice for individual patients.
- Uploading personal health information (PHI).
